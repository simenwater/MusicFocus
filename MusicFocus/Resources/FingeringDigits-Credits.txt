MusicFocus offline fingering recognition — data acknowledgements

The bundled prototype model contains normalized/resampled adaptations of pen
trajectories from the following datasets. It does not contain user handwriting.

Pen-Based Recognition of Handwritten Digits
E. Alpaydin and F. Alimoglu (1996), UCI Machine Learning Repository.
https://doi.org/10.24432/C5MG6K
Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).

UJI Pen Characters (Version 2)
F. Prat, M. Castro, D. Llorens, A. Marzal and J. Vilar (2008), UCI.
https://doi.org/10.24432/C5FG8S
Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).

License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Changes: coordinate normalization, trajectory resampling, digit/non-digit labels,
training subset selection, binary encoding, and confidence/rejection calibration.
The dataset creators do not endorse MusicFocus.

HOMUS was used only for local negative evaluation; its samples and prototypes
are not included in this app.
